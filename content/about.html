Dr. Rakam Lama Tamang is a Senior Geotechnical Project Manager with over 14 years of experience in civil and geotechnical engineering and construction. His work spans investigation, ground improvement, foundation design, and geohazard assessment across complex geological settings and international projects.

He has led and supported projects ranging from small assignments to large design-build infrastructure, coordinated multidisciplinary teams, and mentored junior engineers. His Ph.D. research connects machine learning, physics-informed modeling, and uncertainty quantification with geotechnical practice.

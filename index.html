Rakam Lama Tamang is a Senior Geotechnical Project Manager with over 14 years of experience in civil and geotechnical engineering and construction. His work spans geotechnical investigation, ground improvement, foundation design, and geohazard assessment across complex geological settings and international projects. He has led and supported projects ranging from small assignments to large design-build infrastructure, including mentoring junior engineers.

AI research & engineering intelligence
